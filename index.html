BROADSIDE

A pirate ship, an open sea, and trouble on the horizon.
Checking for VR support…
Quest controls
Left stick: walk the deck
Right stick: snap turn
Grip (side button): grab the wheel, sail lever, muskets, sword
Weapons rack: on your left as you start
Trigger: fire a held musket
Trigger with an empty hand at a cannon's back end: light the fuse
A or X: give the helmsman his next order
Walk away from the wheel and Hawkins steers for you
Desktop controls (for testing)
Click to capture the mouse, WASD to walk
Q / E: turn the wheel, R / F: raise or lower sails
1 musket, 2 sword, 3 empty hands
Click: fire / swing / light the cannon you look at
Space: fire every loaded cannon that has a target
T: next order for the helmsman (he steers when you leave the wheel)
